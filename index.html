Meraku Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

The short version

Meraku keeps your information on your iPhone. It has no accounts, no ads and no tracking, and it does not send your recipes, plans or preferences to the developer or to anyone else.

What stays on your phone

Everything you enter or choose in Meraku is stored only on your device:

  • Your recipes, including names, ingredients, instructions and links
  • Your dinner plan
  • Your preferences, including diets, allergies, favored or hidden cuisines and notes
  • How many people you cook for
  • Which grocery items you have checked off

This information is never sent to the developer. Meraku does not use accounts, sign-in, advertising, analytics or tracking tools. Deleting the app from your iPhone deletes this information.

When Meraku uses the internet

Meraku connects to the internet only when you ask it to:

  • Reading a recipe link. When you paste a link to a recipe, the app opens that web page to read its ingredients. The website you chose can see that request, as it would for any visitor. Meraku does not send your name, plan or preferences with it.
  • Opening other websites. Buttons such as "Search the web instead", "Open recipe" and the store search next to a grocery item open the website or store app in your browser. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

The recipe ideas in Discover come from a library built into the app, so browsing them does not use a server.

Calendar and Reminders

If you choose to add your dinner plan to your calendar or send your grocery list to Reminders, Meraku asks for your permission first. It uses that access only to add those events or reminders when you ask. They are stored in Apple's Calendar and Reminders apps, under your control, and Meraku does not copy them anywhere else. You can turn this access off at any time in your iPhone's Settings.

Feedback

The feedback form opens your own email app with a message ready to send. Nothing is sent until you press send. The email includes the message you wrote, the Meraku version and your iPhone model and iOS version, to help fix problems. We use your email only to read and reply to your feedback.

Children

Meraku is a family dinner planner for adults and does not knowingly collect any information from children. Because the app collects no personal information at all, there is nothing to collect from anyone under 13.

Changes and contact

If Meraku changes in a way that affects your privacy, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change.
